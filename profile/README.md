## Hi there fellow engineers, tinkererers, designers, product people 🖖

Kranus Health is the leading global provider of virtual care in Urology. The company focuses on developing clinically validated therapies for a wide range of indications from Andrology to Uro-Oncology. We are a founding team with a background in high-growth tech companies (e.g. Palantir, Celonis, Amazon) and Professor Dr. Miller from Charité Berlin with offices in Munich and Berlin.

We do this by building digital therapeutics (DTx) that we deliver to our patients/users in the form of mobile applications. We develop our DTx together with our renowned in-house medical team from the Charité in Berlin. 

We have already built 3 clinically validated products (gold-standard RCTs, peer-reviewed in world-renowned journals):
- Kranus Mictera: one therapy for all types of female incontinence. No devices, no waiting times, no side effects.
- Kranus Edera: erectile dysfunction (the first really reimbursable ED root-cause treatment)
- Kranus Lutera: an effective treatment to reduce the frequent urge to urinate, specifically aimed at noticeably relieving symptoms

You can learn more about us and our products on our website.

## Our principles

We have our guiding principles and core beliefs that you hopefully share with us:

1. **First Principles** – We dig deep, stripping problems to their core truths until we uncover ground truth.​
2. **Go where the pain is** – We run toward the hardest problems, treating failure as our fastest teacher.​
3. **High agency** – We step up, decide and own our work to deliver results.​
4. **Beginner's mind** – We stay curious and humble, always ready to question assumptions and learn anew.​
5. **Disagree & commit** – We debate openly, decide decisively, then align fully to execute at speed.​
6. **Focus** – We nail our critical priorities and deliberately drop the rest.​

## The Stack

Cloud-native through and through, now accelerated through AI across the entire org.

- AI apps (on-prem): vLLM (currently w. Gemma models), LiteLLM, Langfuse, PydanticAI
- AI apps (cloud): Gemini + plain vanilla agent specs and FastAPI
- Infra: GCP, k8s everything + ArgoCD & kustomize, OpenTofu, DD, Teleport
- Backend (monorepo): FastAPI, PGDB, Redis
- Mobile apps (monorepo): Flutter
  
(+ a bunch of other stuff like React, TS, ... and we use GitHub (surprise!) & GitHub Actions for CI & CD)
